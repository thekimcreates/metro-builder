# Milestone 4 — Unified Builder Wand Testing

1. Hold the Builder Wand in Creative Mode.
2. Right-click empty space once. A pending PSD should appear with a white outline.
3. Press `[` and `]`; each press must rotate the pending PSD exactly -1° or +1°.
4. Press the arrow keys; the pending PSD should move 0.01 blocks in its local directions.
5. Hold Shift while pressing an arrow key; movement should be 0.001 blocks.
6. Use Page Up/Page Down to move vertically.
7. Press `P`, edit X/Y/Z/Yaw, and Save.
8. Right-click another spot. The pending PSD should be placed there and no new preview should appear automatically.
9. Right-click once more at empty space. A new pending PSD should appear and inherit the last placed PSD's pack, rotation, and scale.
10. Right-click an existing PSD. Its properties panel should open and it should receive a white outline.
11. Close the world and reopen it. Placed PSD positions and rotations must persist.
